# What can LOKI do?
<!-- tags: examples, gallery, ideas, tasks, help with -->

Open **What can LOKI do?** (the grid icon at the bottom left, or **More ideas…** on the home screen). It has 32 examples in 8 groups:

- **Everyday life** - meal plans, polite messages, packing lists, simple explanations.
- **Work** - emails, meeting agendas, putting tasks in order, a stronger CV.
- **Study** - study plans, quizzes, explaining a topic, essay outlines.
- **Creative** - stories, poems, name ideas, drawing ideas.
- **Home** - cleaning schedules, small repairs, plant care, moving house.
- **Money** - budgets, saving tips, comparing choices, money words explained.
- **Health info** - sleep, stretching, questions for your doctor, quick healthy meals (general information only - see *Health information and safety*).
- **Small business** - product descriptions, social posts, replying to customers, a one-page plan.

Press one and LOKI does it straight away in a new chat, in **Ask** mode, so nothing on your computer changes. Then change the words to make it yours ("make it shorter", "for four people", "in a friendlier tone").

## Beyond the examples

Just ask in your own words. LOKI can also:

- read and summarise documents you give it,
- write and fix code, and run it to check it works (Build mode),
- search the web and cite its sources (when a web tool is set up),
- make pictures (Settings > Creations),
- run longer jobs in the background while you keep chatting (**Run in background** under the message box).

Search the examples with the box at the top, or pick a group with the buttons under it.
