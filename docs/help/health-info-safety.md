# Health information and safety
<!-- tags: health, medical, doctor, emergency, safety, symptoms -->

LOKI can give **general health information**: tips for sleep, gentle exercise, healthy meals, and help preparing questions for your doctor.

LOKI is **not a doctor**. It cannot examine you, it can be wrong, and it does not know your full medical history.

- **In an emergency, call your local emergency number** (for example 112 in Europe, 911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK, 108/112 in India).
- For symptoms that are severe, sudden, getting worse or worrying you, contact a doctor - do not wait for an AI answer.
- Do not start, stop or change a medicine because of what LOKI says. Ask a doctor or pharmacist.
- If you are thinking about harming yourself, please contact a local crisis line or emergency services now, or talk to someone you trust.

The health examples in **What can LOKI do?** carry this note, and they ask LOKI to keep the answer safe and to say when to see a doctor.
