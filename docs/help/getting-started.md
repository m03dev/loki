# Getting started with LOKI
<!-- tags: start, first, welcome, begin, new, tour -->

LOKI is a helper that lives on your computer. You talk to it in your own words, in your own language, and it answers, writes, plans and explains. It can also work on files for you, but it always asks before doing anything risky.

## The first five minutes

1. **Pick your language.** The welcome tour asks first. You can change it later in **Settings > General > Language and accessibility**.
2. **Connect a free model.** LOKI needs an AI model to think with. Choose one:
   - **On this computer** - private, free, works without internet. LOKI installs Ollama and downloads a model that fits your computer.
   - **A free online model** - fast on any computer. Sign in with OpenRouter, or paste a free key from Groq or Google Gemini.
3. **Say what you want help with.** LOKI shows matching examples first.
4. **Try an example.** Each one runs in a new chat and changes nothing on your computer.

## Asking LOKI something

Click **Loki** in the middle of the home screen (or just start typing), write your message and press **Enter**. Press **Shift+Enter** for a new line.

## The three modes

Under the message box you can choose how much LOKI may do:

- **Ask** - it answers questions and changes nothing.
- **Plan** - it reads and writes a plan, but changes nothing.
- **Build** - it edits files, runs things and checks its own work, and asks you before anything risky.

New to LOKI? Stay in **Ask**. The examples in **What can LOKI do?** always use Ask.

## Where to find things

- **What can LOKI do?** (the grid icon at the bottom left) - 32 examples to run with one click.
- **?** (top right) or **F1** - this help.
- **Settings** (the gear) - models, language, accessibility, add-ons and more.

Missed the welcome tour? Open **Settings > General** and press **Show the welcome tour again**.
