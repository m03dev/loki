# Privacy and safety
<!-- tags: privacy, private, data, keys, safe, approval, permission, risky, offline -->

## What stays on your computer

- Your chats, LOKI's memory, your settings and your keys are kept on this computer (in LOKI's data folder). Keys are never shown again after you paste them.
- With a **local model** (Ollama / MLX), nothing you ask leaves your computer.
- Want to be sure? Turn on **Settings > General > Private: only models on this computer**.

## What goes online

- With an **online model** (OpenRouter, Groq, Gemini...), what you ask is sent to that service so it can answer. Read their terms; some free tiers may use prompts to improve their models.
- Web searches go to the search service LOKI uses.
- Updates download new versions of LOKI.

## LOKI asks before risky things

Deleting files, spending money, signing in, posting or sending anything always asks you first. You see what LOKI wants to do and press **Allow** or **Deny**. In **Ask** and **Plan** modes LOKI changes nothing at all.

## Money

LOKI uses free and local models first. Paid providers are off unless you allow them in Settings > General, and then only up to the daily limit you set. **Settings > Usage** shows what every message cost.
