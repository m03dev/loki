# When something does not work
<!-- tags: problem, error, broken, not working, fix, slow, white window, no answer, troubleshooting -->

## LOKI does not answer

- The top bar says what is wrong. If no model is connected, see **Connect a free AI model**.
- An online model may have reached its free daily limit. Connect a second one (Settings > Connections) - LOKI switches by itself.
- A local model may still be downloading: Settings > Local models shows the progress.

## Check everything

Press **Check everything** (the shield icon at the bottom left). LOKI tests its parts on this computer and says, for anything that does not work, exactly what to do.

## The window is blank or shows a rescue screen

Quit LOKI and open it again. If an update caused it, LOKI goes back to the version that worked by itself, and **Settings > Machines & updates** says why.

## It is slow

- Local models are slower on computers without a strong graphics card or Apple Silicon. A free online model is faster.
- Turn on **Reduce motion** (Settings > General) to stop animations.

## Still stuck?

Ask LOKI: "LOKI, why can't you answer?" - it can explain its own state. Or note the message you see and the steps you took, and ask whoever gave you LOKI.
