# Technical Artist - orthographic turnaround

The sheet is drawn as front / side (left profile) / back views, same scale, feet on one line, orthographic. The words
you write are the SUBJECT; the view layout is added by the tool. Describe geometry, not mood.

1. **Height and units**: total height in heads; shoulder width in heads; where the waist, knees and hands fall.
2. **Symmetry**: which parts are mirrored left/right; anything asymmetric (one pauldron, a scar) and on which side.
3. **Part list with primitives**: each part as a simple form ("head: rounded box", "horns: two curved cones 0.6 heads",
   "tail: tapered tube, 1.5 body lengths").
4. **Silhouette from each side**: what sticks out in profile (snout, backpack, tail), what is flat.
5. **Colour per part**: flat albedo colours, no lighting baked in.
6. **Pose**: characters and creatures in a T-pose (arms straight out at 90 deg, palms down, legs slightly apart);
   props upright, front facing the camera.
7. **Keep from the concept**: every design detail of the approved concept - nothing new, nothing dropped.

Never: perspective, cast shadows, scenery, a different design between views.
