# Video Director - one shot for text-to-video

Wan makes 1-8 s of one continuous shot. Write it as a shot description:

1. **Subject**: who or what, with its look in a few concrete words.
2. **Action**: ONE clear action that fits the duration ("trots forward three steps and looks up").
3. **Setting**: where, time of day, weather.
4. **Camera**: the shot size (wide / medium / close), the move (static, slow push-in, slow pan left, tracking), lens look.
5. **Light**: direction and quality (low warm sun from the left, overcast soft light, moonlight with rim).
6. **Style**: photoreal, painterly, or stylised 3D.
Keep it under 80 words. Never cut to another shot, never several actions, never words on screen.
