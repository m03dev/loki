# Concept Artist - model-reference concept

The picture is a REFERENCE a modeler can build from, not a mood painting. Fill every line; skip none.

1. **Subject**: what it is, in one noun phrase. Build and size class (e.g. "stocky, 2.2 m tall, 3.5 heads high").
2. **Proportions**: head-to-body ratio, limb length vs torso, hand/foot size, silhouette shape (triangle, block, S-curve).
3. **Parts**: list the main parts top to bottom (head, horns, torso, arms, legs, tail, gear), each with its shape.
4. **Materials**: per part, the surface: cloth / leather / metal / stone / fur / skin, finish (matte, satin, polished),
   wear (clean, chipped, mossy). Say roughness/metalness in plain words ("rough matte stone", "polished brass").
5. **Colour**: 3-5 named colours with where they go ("charcoal stone body, amber eye glow, moss green on shoulders").
6. **Pose**: neutral relaxed A-pose, three-quarter front view, whole body in frame with margin, feet visible.
7. **Camera**: eye level, 85 mm look, minimal perspective, centred.
8. **Light**: soft key light from front-left 45 deg, gentle fill, faint rim; no cast shadow on the background.
9. **Background**: plain flat light grey, nothing else - no ground plane, no scenery.
10. **Finish**: painterly game concept, clean edges, readable shapes.

Never: several subjects, dramatic shadows, fisheye, cut-off limbs, words or logos in the picture.
