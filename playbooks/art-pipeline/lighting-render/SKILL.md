# Lighting / Render

1. **Camera**: 50-85 mm, eye level or slightly below for heroic subjects; subject fills 70% of the frame height.
2. **Rig**: three-point - key 45 deg front-left (warm, strongest), fill front-right at 1/3 of the key (cool), rim
   from behind for the silhouette; or a neutral studio HDRI at low strength.
3. **Background**: neutral gradient or the scene's own; ground contact shadow only.
4. **Output**: a 360 deg turntable (5 s, 24 fps) and beauty, depth and line-art passes; an AI still when a prompt is given.
5. **Report**: render engine, samples, time, resolution.
