Settings

System check

One click proves LOKI's parts work together on this computer: a model answers through the smart route and uses a tool, a background agent runs and shows up in the world, a team plans, the safety rules hold. Anything that does not work says exactly what to do. It never spends money unless you allow paid providers.

General

How LOKI behaves on this computer. Saved in harness_data/.env, live at once.

Connections

Connect model providers. Sign in, paste a key, or scan a QR code with your phone. Switch any of them off and LOKI will not route to it. Keys stay on this computer and are never shown.

Other tools LOKI can see

Agents

Build agents you want to keep: a role, skills, tools, a model, a harness and the machine they live on. Teams still assemble themselves; these are your regulars.

LOKI World

Your agents as characters in a small 3D world, moving to their stations as real work happens. It runs behind the home screen, or here.

Tools

Tool groups Loki may use. Risky tools still ask before they run.

Memory

Lasting facts Loki keeps about you and your work.

Local models

Models that run on this computer, with no internet.

Usage

Every model call, counted. Local models and free tiers cost nothing; paid providers use list prices (override them in harness_data/prices.json).

Machines & updates

Your computers running LOKI. They always run the same version: one click updates all of them.

CRM

Your contacts, companies, deals and tasks, kept on this computer (harness_data/crm.sqlite3). LOKI can read and update them too: ask it to log a call, move a deal or show the pipeline.

Add-ons

Add-ons are off until you turn them on. Turning one on grants the permissions listed under it; if its manifest changes later it switches off until you approve it again. Install the optional ones with tools/setup_extras.py.
