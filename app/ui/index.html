Ask a question, plan a change, or switch to Build and let Loki edit, run and verify its own work — asking you before anything risky.

Settings

System check

One click proves LOKI's parts work together on this computer: a model answers through the smart route and uses a tool, a background agent runs and shows up in the world, a team plans, the safety rules hold. Anything that does not work says exactly what to do. It never spends money unless you allow paid providers.

General

How LOKI behaves on this computer. Saved in harness_data/.env, live at once.

Connections

Connect model providers. Sign in, paste a key, or scan a QR code with your phone. Switch any of them off and LOKI will not route to it. Keys stay on this computer and are never shown.


        

        
Other tools LOKI can see

      

      

        
Agents
Build agents you want to keep: a role, skills, tools, a model, a harness and the machine they live on. Teams still assemble themselves; these are your regulars.

        

      

      

        
LOKI World
The Godot game of your agents and their real work. Edit it in the Godot editor (Godot tab > Open in Godot); "Rebuild the World tab" brings your changes here.

        

        

      

      

        
Automations
LOKI does things by itself: on a schedule, when a file lands in a folder, a new email, before a meeting, a webhook, a word on Telegram, a job ending or a machine coming online. Say it in one sentence; you see the card and turn it on. Anything that touches your accounts asks first unless you pre-approve that exact automation.

        

      

      

        
Tools
Everything LOKI's agents can touch: does it work, what is missing, and the fix. Tools that cannot work right now are hidden from the model. Risky actions still ask before they run.

Memory
What LOKI remembers about you, your projects, people, preferences, decisions and past work - the same on every linked machine. Check what it learned, undo what it got wrong.

        

        

          Your notes for LOKI (always included)
          
          

        

      

      

        
Local models
Models that run on this computer, with no internet.

        

      

      

        
Usage
Every model call, counted. Local models and free tiers cost nothing; paid providers use list prices (override them in harness_data/prices.json).

        

        

      

      

        
Your data
What LOKI keeps on this computer and where, which model providers got your messages, backups you can restore, and clearing what you do not want kept.

        

      

      

        
Machines & updates
Your computers running LOKI. They always run the same version: one click updates all of them.

        

        

      

      

        
Creations
Everything LOKI made for you - pictures, videos, sketches, music, voice and games - newest first. Ask for the next one here, in a style; LOKI makes it in the background, on this computer once local-image is installed (Add-ons), else with the free online maker. Star your favourites: the daily picture learns from them.

        

      

      

        
Brain
Everything LOKI knows, as one map: LOKI and its specialists, their tools, memory, skills, recent jobs, your project's docs and your Obsidian vault (read only), joined by their links and #tags.

        

      

      

        
Play
Games made with LOKI's own Pixel Engine, in LOKI World's pixel cel-shaded look. Ask LOKI to make one ("make a game where the fox...") and it appears here. Games run sealed off from LOKI: they cannot see your files or keys.

        

      

      

        
Lab - LOKI grows itself
LOKI builds its own personal-agent core here, one change at a time, when it is idle: your ideas first, then the roadmap in docs/EVOLVE.md. Each change is made in its own copy of the code, tested on every suite and shipped only if all pass; what worked and what did not is kept and used for the next try.

        

        

      

      

        
CRM
Your contacts, companies, deals and tasks, kept on this computer (harness_data/crm.sqlite3). LOKI can read and update them too: ask it to log a call, move a deal or show the pipeline.

        

      

      

        
Add-ons
Add-ons are off until you turn them on. Turning one on grants the permissions listed under it; if its manifest changes later it switches off until you approve it again. Install the optional ones with tools/setup_extras.py.
